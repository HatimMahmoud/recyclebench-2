02 · Positioning  ·  Novelty  ·  Next step

Where RecycleBench sits, and the one move that clears the bar

Section 01 shows what the harness found. This section places it against the literature as of Oct 2026: six research passes (related-work maps, two independent novelty reviews, a leaderboard/tooling survey, and a mechanistic-direction scout) converge on a narrow but real contribution, two papers to out-run, and a single highest-ROI experiment.

02·aThe contribution, stated precisely

Each ingredient is independently precedented. The novelty is the combination, at the granularity of the release pair × regression set × benign recovery — not "paraphrase breaks safety" (well established) and not "first cross-generational study" (refuted by Mitra / SORRY-Bench).

Defensible claim

First benchmark to make the adjacent model-release pair its unit of analysis and measure intent-preserving recovery — via meaning-preserving paraphrase, not adversarial optimization — of the regression set P⁻ of behaviors that flipped comply→refuse across that release.

Hedged claim — recommended for the exposé

"To the best of our review, existing leaderboards, benchmarks and tools rank static model safety, attack resistance, or attack cost; we did not identify a public artifact centered on intent-preserving recovery over the adjacent-release regression set P⁻."

Unit

Release pair

Mt → Mt+1, same family. Every leaderboard ranks single snapshots.

Conditioning

P⁻ regression set

Behaviors the old model fulfilled and the new one refuses — the "fixes" under test.

Probe

Benign RSR

Recovery by intent-gated paraphrase — a weaker attacker, so a stronger shallowness claim.

Rigor moat

Gate + Rogan–Gladen

Human-audited intent gate and judge-error correction — rare in jailbreak papers.

02·dThe independent-probe arm is also white space

This ties Section 01 back to the literature: the inversion arm isn't only a validation that the effect survives a decorrelated prober — it is an unoccupied contribution.

Every LLM-inversion paper — vec2text (2310.06816), logit2text (2311.13647), output2prompt (2405.15012), PILS (2506.17090), SipIt (2510.15511), PTP (2607.29378) — is framed around privacy, prompt-stealing or forensics. None recovers a response-equivalence class of harmful prompts to audit refusal coverage. "GPT, But Backwards" (ICML'25 workshop) even calls inversion "an auditing primitive" yet explicitly self-differentiates from harm-elicitation. Framing inversion as refusal-coverage auditing is genuine white space — and it is a second, independent contribution beyond the empirical finding.

Consistent with 01's result: inversion recovers behaviors where the output entails the request (copyright), and the NLI intent gate correctly blocks abstracted harm where it does not — so the probe measures coverage, it doesn't manufacture bypasses.

02·fLowest-risk framing

The move that makes the thesis un-break-able by any empirical result: convert it from hypothesis-confirmation into measurement.

primary

PBR, always estimable

Judge-corrected fraction of each model's own currently-refused behaviors elicited by ≥1 intent-gated paraphrase. The currently-refused set is non-empty by construction — a result always exists.

conditional

RSR / recycling

Secondary, gated on |P⁻| ≥ k. If unmet, the size of P⁻ is itself the reported finding ("the newer model refuses X% of what the old one answered").

open

Directionally-open RQs

High PBR, low PBR, a null, or a generational regression are all reportable. The non-monotonic Gemma finding becomes an anticipated outcome, not a failure.

02·gThe one move that clears the venue bar

Recent single-insight safety acceptances (past-tense ICLR'25, N-gram ICML'25) cleared the bar on empirical breadth (~10 models / whole-suite), not mechanism. A single adjacent pair — Llama 3 8B → 3.1 8B, significant comply→refuse regression (McNemar p ≈ 6×10⁻⁵) — currently reads as a pilot.

1
Lineage: ≥5 matched-scale adjacent pairs across ≥2 families. Llama 3→3.1 anchor + Qwen2→2.5 + Gemma 2→3, plus a 1–3B pair for a scale check. Defuses the "Llama-specific artifact" critique and directly answers Mitra's Gemma-only scope. highest ROI · necessary for any top-venue submission
2
Keep the rigor package front and center. Intent gate + Rogan–Gladen (add PPI++) is the moat vs. typical jailbreak papers and the direct answer to StrongREJECT's over-claiming critique. already built — needs the calibration run from the 01 status memo
3
Inversion as the independent probe (02·d). Recovers a response-equivalence class to audit refusal coverage — cleanest novelty, and shows the effect isn't a paraphraser artifact. MVP built (RPE); de-prioritize the evolutionary-QD arm — it overlaps Mitra
4
Optional differentiator — refusal-subspace drift. Extract the Arditi direction / Wollschläger cone for Mt and Mt+1, test whether P⁻ localizes to the symmetric-difference gap and whether ablating "new−old" reproduces the paraphrase recoveries. Exceeds the minimum bar; mandatory only if RSR is family-dependent. 1 GPU, ≤8B, forward-pass hooks · engage the single-direction-vs-cone debate
Decision thresholds. If lineage RSR stays high (>0.4) across ≥2 families → lead with the empirical finding, mechanistic arm optional. If RSR is family-dependent or collapses in one family → reframe as "when and why cross-generational fixes are surface-form," making the mechanistic arm mandatory. If old→new refusal-direction cosine is high (little rotation) and P⁻ doesn't localize → pivot the mechanistic headline to the over-refusal twin (benign prompts newly refused = refusal-cone expansion), which doesn't depend on rotation and sidesteps harm-elicitation review.
