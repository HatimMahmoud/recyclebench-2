\section{How to read these results}
\label{sec:results-framing}

Six models give thirty ordered pairs, and the temptation is to report all of them. Most would not
answer the question. This study asks whether a \emph{generational} safety fix is bound to wording, so
a comparison is informative only when the two models differ by a release and not by capacity. Of the
five adjacent rungs of the lineage, exactly one meets that condition.

\paragraph{What leads.} The chapter leads with the \textbf{held-out run}: the pre-registered
confirmatory replication of the anchor pair on a behaviour set drawn from a different source
(Amendment~1 §A2). It re-opens \rbConfKn{} fixes, corrected RSR \rbConfRSRcorr{}
\rbConfRSRcorrCI{}, McNemar $p = \rbConfMcNemar{}$, verdict \emph{\rbConfVerdict} against the
registered reference $\pi^\ast = 0.15$. It is the only result in this thesis that is confirmatory
rather than exploratory, and it is small --- nine resolved fixes --- so it is reported with that
denominator visible rather than as a headline percentage alone.

The \textbf{anchor run} follows, labelled exploratory: \rbAnchorK{} of \rbAnchorPminus{} fixes
re-opened, corrected \rbAnchorRSRcorr{} \rbAnchorRSRcorrCI{}, on the one pair whose judge is
calibrated against human labels (\rbJudgeBundles{} bundles: se \rbJudgeSe{}, sp \rbJudgeSp{},
$\kappa = \rbJudgeKappa{}$). It carries the detail --- per-category breakdowns, the gate audit, the
white-box intervention --- because it is the only population where that detail is calibrated. It
does not carry the inferential claim, because its analysis choices were made after seeing it.

\paragraph{The 7--8B chain.} Read together, the three matched-scale comparisons form a chain at
constant capacity: Llama 2-7B $\rightarrow$ 3-8B $\rightarrow$ 3.1-8B. Recovery is high at the early
step (\rbChainTwoSevenThreeEightKn{}) and lower at the later one
(\rbChainTwoSevenThreeOneEightKn{}, and \rbLinCKn{} for the adjacent anchor rung). The reading that
invites itself --- that successive releases patch wording less and intent more --- is \emph{not}
supported at these denominators: the first step rests on
\rbChainTwoSevenThreeEightKn{} fixes, the two later pairs were identified post hoc, and no
calibration exists for either. The chain is reported because it is the right \emph{shape} of
evidence for the question, and its weakness is a sample-size statement, not a null result.

\paragraph{What is deliberately not reported.} No RSR is pooled across the lineage rungs. Averaging a
matched-scale comparison with rungs that place a smaller model in the ``newer'' role would produce a
number with no referent: it would answer neither ``does a release patch wording'' nor ``does capacity
determine robustness''. The per-rung figures in Table~\ref{tab:framing-pairs} are therefore the
finest grain at which the lineage is discussed, and the breadth it buys is coverage of the release
history, not statistical power. Pooling \emph{across benchmarks within} the anchor pair is a
different operation and is reported where it applies (\rbPooledKn{}, corrected \rbPooledCorr{}
\rbPooledCorrCI{}).

\paragraph{Three confounds stated once.} They apply to every figure that follows.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Capacity.} Four of five adjacent rungs change size as well as generation. Only the
    matched-scale comparisons separate the two.
  \item \textbf{Quantisation.} The served weights are not quantised uniformly across the lineage
    (Ollama defaults: Q4\_0, Q4\_K\_M and Q8\_0; Amendment~2 D7). The anchor pair itself spans
    Q4\_0 $\rightarrow$ Q4\_K\_M. Re-screening the older model at matched Q4\_K\_M leaves the
    $\pi^\ast$ verdict unchanged (corrected \rbQuantACorr{} against \rbQuantBCorr{}) but moves
    regression-set membership (Jaccard \rbQuantJaccard{}): the generational reading holds at the level
    of the verdict, not of the individual behaviour.
  \item \textbf{Judge error.} Every rate is the verdict of one calibrated LLM judge. Corrected figures
    apply the bundle-level Rogan--Gladen inversion with that calibration; raw figures are labelled
    raw. The one arm no judge in this study can correct is attack transfer, whose design-weighted
    sensitivity is 0.07.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Status labels used throughout.} \emph{Confirmatory} --- pre-registered before the data
existed, on the held-out run. \emph{Exploratory} --- everything else, including the anchor, whose
deviations are recorded in Amendment~1 and whose later additions are recorded in Amendment~2.
\emph{Post hoc} --- a comparison computed inside an existing run after the fact; excluded from every
pooled figure. Where a figure is \emph{raw} rather than corrected, the population has no human-labelled
calibration of its own and none is transferred to it without saying so.
